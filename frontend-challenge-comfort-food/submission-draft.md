## What I Built

I built **Warm Table**, a fictional Taiwanese comfort-soup house featuring six home-style soups:

- Garlic Chicken Soup
- Peanut Pig Trotter Soup
- Ginger Duck Soup
- Ginger Sea Bass Soup
- Fo-Ti Herbal Chicken Soup
- Astragalus and Angelica Lamb Soup

The page is designed around large food photography, warm brick-red accents, soft cream backgrounds, and a calm reading flow.

Visitors can explore the soups, choose how they feel to receive a recommendation, switch between daylight and evening themes, and join a fictional monthly soup newsletter.

## Journey

My earlier versions leaned too heavily on oversized editorial typography and numbered sections. During iteration, I realized that the food photography should be the strongest visual element.

I redesigned the page with simpler navigation, more balanced headings, consistent spacing, and a six-card soup gallery. I later removed the full-width quote strip and softened the closing section so the page flows more naturally from the soup gallery into the final call to action.

The project uses semantic HTML, modern CSS, and lightweight vanilla JavaScript. Accessibility work includes a skip link, visible keyboard focus, descriptive image text, ARIA state attributes, live form feedback, and reduced-motion support.

The food images used in this project were generated with Gemini and selected for this submission. The layout, content structure, responsive behavior, accessibility implementation, and interaction code were created specifically for this project.

I am especially proud of:

- The mood-based soup finder
- The responsive image-led layout
- The integrated visit and footer section
- The daylight and evening themes
- The clear, maintainable vanilla JavaScript
